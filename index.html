KAYLEE-JANE

AGENT · ACTRESS · ARTIST

Unconventional by nature. curious by default.

I'm Kaylee-Jane — a South African actor, creator, artist and agent with an expressive personality, an enquiring mind and a particular fascination with people.

I'm interested in what makes us tick, what we believe, why we behave the way we do and all the contradictions that make being human so wonderfully complicated.

My world sits somewhere between entertainment and observation, humour and serious thought, performance and the unexpected.

I question things. I laugh a lot. I have a habit of looking at something from a perspective that perhaps wasn't immediately obvious.

And I'm perfectly comfortable with all of those things existing in the same person.

And now I represent other people who are exactly like that.

As an agent, I see the dimension in my talent them apart, that others may oversee and I make sure it gets seen and protected.

MORE THAN ONE DIMENSION.

I'm naturally expressive, outspoken, curious and rarely short of something to say.

I've always been interested in what sits beneath the surface: why people behave the way they do, how we form our beliefs, what shapes our identities, why we conform and what happens when we start questioning the things we've always accepted.

I'm not particularly interested in your final answer. I want to know how you got there. What did you assume? What aren't you seeing? What happens when you turn the whole thing upside down?

My curiosity extends well beyond human behaviour. I'm fascinated by consciousness, spirituality, the universe, energy, existence, creativity and the questions we don't necessarily have answers to.

And then there's the other side of me.

I love making people laugh just as much as I enjoy making them think. Sometimes those two things happen in the same breath. I can be discussing the nature of existence one minute and laughing at something completely ridiculous the next.

I don't see those things as contradictions. They're all part of being human.

I bring that curiosity and personality into everything I do — whether I'm stepping into a character, creating something, speaking directly to a camera or exploring an idea.

And now I bring it into the people I represent.

As an Agent, I do for my talent what I've always done for myself: I see the dimension other people miss. I see the story underneath the person. I protect it, I pitch it.

My job is to make room for them to be fully themselves, while I handle the business, the negotiations, and the noise around them.

I'm comfortable standing by what I believe, but I'm equally comfortable questioning my own thinking. I don't need everyone to agree with me, and I don't believe disagreement needs to become a personal attack.

I'm not here to manufacture a personality. I'm here to express one — mine, and theirs.

A CHARACTER IS AN OPPORTUNITY TO EXPLORE ANOTHER DIMENSION OF HUMAN BEHAVIOUR.

I'm the girl you cast when you want the audience to laugh and feel slightly unsafe at the same time.

I have a particular appreciation for formidable women. The big boss. The unpredictable one. The woman who runs the room, ruins your life, or both.

Funny, feral, fearless and impossible to ignore.

I enjoy characters with complexity, a sharp edge, a questionable moral compass or an unexpected sense of humour. Characters that give an actor something to explore rather than simply something to play.

I'm particularly drawn to improvisation, spontaneity and expressive performance. I enjoy the unexpected moments — the expressions, reactions and little details that make a character distinctive, believable and memorable.

I'm interested in comedy and drama, and in exploring the full range of human behaviour through performance.

Sometimes the audience should laugh. Sometimes they should be uncomfortable. Occasionally, both at once.

If she's the psycho you secretly love, the boss you don't mess with, or the woman everyone remembers, I'm interested.

IDEAS, OBSERVATIONS AND A LITTLE UNPREDICTABILITY.

My creative work brings together humour, personal observations, independent thinking and an interest in the human experience.

Some days it's a funny moment. Other days it's an uncomfortable question, an unexpected perspective or an observation about something most people overlook.

I'm interested in people, relationships, human behaviour, spirituality, consciousness, everyday life, personal experiences and the wonderfully absurd things we sometimes take far too seriously.

I also enjoy exploring ideas through visual expression, creative experiments, storytelling and original content.

I don't want to be confined to one subject, one format or one version of myself.

The content can be serious, funny, thought-provoking, spontaneous or completely unexpected.

The connecting thread is always the same: my perspective, my personality and my way of expressing them.

No manufactured character. No artificial perfection. Just personality, perspective and expression.
