About Franko.

ARTUR CZEKAJ — RYBA HYBRYD AKA PACOZZIPOLACCO — is a South African-based actor, music creator, producer and songwriter with a presence you cannot ignore.

He is raw, magnetic and unforgettable — the man audiences remember long after the scene ends. His breakout screen role as FRANKO in NEW PRISON (Thando Mseleku Films) put him on the map as a performer who brings real emotion, real edge and real story to every character.

Artur’s work sits between acting, music and visual art. He is not polished and pretend. He is real, raw and cross-cultural — and that is exactly why people believe him on camera.

As an artist, he creates from lived experience. As an actor, he is drawn to complex, formidable, unforgettable characters — the boss, the unpredictable one, the man who runs the room or ruins your life, or both.

He is the Founder of MENTAL MENTOR STUDIO — his creative company for music, production and storytelling.
