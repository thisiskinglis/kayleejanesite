About Franko.

Artur Czecaj, known as Ryba Hybryd and as Franko on screen, is a South African-based actor, artist and music creator with a presence you cannot ignore.

He is raw, magnetic and unforgettable — the man audiences remember long after the scene ends. His breakout role as Franko in NEW PRISON / Female Prison (Thando Mseleku Films) put him on the map as a performer who brings real emotion, real edge and real story to every character.

Artur’s work sits between acting, music and visual art. He is not polished and pretend. He is real, raw and cross-cultural — and that is exactly why people believe him on camera.

As an artist, he creates from lived experience. As an actor, he is drawn to complex, formidable, unforgettable characters — the boss, the unpredictable one, the man who runs the room or ruins your life, or both.
