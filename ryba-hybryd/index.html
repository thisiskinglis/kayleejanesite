Meet Artur.

One person. Several creative languages. No interest in being reduced to just one.

Artur Czekaj is a South African-based actor, music creator, producer and songwriter. Working under the names RYBA HYBRYD and PACOZZIPOLACCO, he moves between screen performance, original music and independent creative production.

His screen work includes the role of Franko in New Prison, a Thando Mseleku Films production. It is a character-led role that forms part of his growing screen portfolio.

His creative interests extend beyond acting. Music, production and storytelling are connected parts of his practice, allowing him to approach a project from more than one creative position.

Artur is also the founder of MENTAL MENTOR STUDIO, his creative platform for music, production and storytelling.

His signature says it plainly: NO DEAL. JUST REAL.

On screen.

Character, contradiction and the space between what a person shows and what they keep hidden.

Artur is interested in characters with an edge — individuals whose presence, choices and contradictions give a scene something to work with.

His casting potential includes dramatic roles, dark comedy, complex supporting characters and personalities that sit outside the predictable.

  • Character-led performance: Roles with a strong identity, point of view or distinctive presence.
  • Comedy & drama: Opportunities across contrasting tones and character types.
  • Improvisation: Open to spontaneous, expressive and creatively exploratory work.
  • Screen presence: A distinctive look and performance identity for character-driven productions.
