MITCH • KAYLEE-JANE TALENT

Meet Mitch.

For Mitch, food is about more than what's on the plate.

He's a chef with a generous approach to food and a personality that feels at home around a kitchen table. He's the kind of person who thinks about whether everyone has eaten, remembers the little things and understands that a good meal can bring people together.

His style is grounded rather than showy. Think proper comfort food, flavour, fire, familiar favourites and meals made to be enjoyed — not simply photographed.

Away from the plate, Mitch brings a relaxed, warm presence that lends itself naturally to food-led storytelling, hospitality environments and on-camera work.

What makes him interesting: the combination of culinary ability, approachable personality and a calm energy that gives people room to enjoy the experience.

His approach.

No kitchen theatrics. Just good food and a genuine love of the process.

  • Grounded — approachable, relaxed and comfortable in his own skin
  • Food-led — a chef whose connection to food is central to his work
  • Warm — a natural fit for content built around people, meals and shared experiences
  • Calm — an understated presence in the kitchen and on camera
  • Versatile — from everyday cooking and braai culture to food campaigns and hospitality projects

Why Mitch?

  • He has a natural connection to food: His strongest positioning comes from his identity as a chef, not from pretending to be an influencer.
  • He feels approachable: His calm, warm manner suits content where audiences want to feel included rather than sold to.
  • He brings a different energy: Not every food personality needs to be loud, fast or theatrical.
  • He has room to create: From practical cooking content to more considered food and hospitality storytelling.
  • He connects food with people: The meal, the kitchen and the company around the table are all part of the story.
