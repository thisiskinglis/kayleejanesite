About Mitch.

Mitch is 36, but he looks 26 — and he cooks like a man who’s been feeding people his whole life.

He’s a normal, huge-hearted human. The kind of guy who remembers how you take your coffee, who checks if you’ve eaten, who’ll stay late to make sure everyone’s fed and happy.

And then he cooks. And you get it. This man loves food. Not for show. For people.

His food is real food — the kind you want seconds of. Comfort, but done properly. Fire, flavour, soul. He’s not plating tiny dots for Instagram. He’s making food that makes people close their eyes and go “mmm.”

He’s photogenic without trying. Young face, warm eyes, easy smile. He looks 26 on camera, but he carries himself like a man who’s seen life and chose kindness anyway.
