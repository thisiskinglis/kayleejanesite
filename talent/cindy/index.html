Meet Cindy.

Confident, feminine and entirely comfortable taking up space.

Cindy has a distinctly South African warmth and an expressive personality. She's the woman who laughs openly, speaks with animation when something interests her and brings her own character into a room.

Her look is versatile: long dark hair, striking eyes and a naturally feminine silhouette. She can carry a relaxed, minimal look just as comfortably as a more considered fashion or evening aesthetic.

There is a place for her in campaigns that recognise women as they are — confident, individual, varied in shape and experience, and uninterested in disappearing into someone else's idea of beauty.

Cindy's distinction is not simply how she looks. It is the combination of her appearance, expression, personality and the confidence she brings to a visual brief.

What makes her interesting: a mature, expressive presence with the range to move between everyday lifestyle, sophisticated fashion and character-led imagery.

Her presence.

Sophisticated when the brief calls for it. Warm, expressive and unmistakably herself when it doesn't.

  • Elegance — polished without feeling distant
  • Confidence — comfortable in her own appearance and presence
  • Warmth — an expressive personality with a distinctly human quality
  • Versatility — natural, lifestyle, fashion and more elevated visual styles
  • Character — a face and presence with room for storytelling
