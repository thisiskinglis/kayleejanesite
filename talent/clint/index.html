Meet Clint.

More than meets the eye. And the eye gets a lot.

Clint brings together a distinctive appearance, an understated presence and an ability to articulate ideas with thought and substance.

His appeal lies in that combination: visual individuality, communication and perspective. Visual pull, real personality, and something worth saying.
