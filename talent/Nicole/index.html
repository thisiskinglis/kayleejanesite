NICOLE · KAYLEE-JANE

Meet Nicole.

Some people bring a look. Others bring a point of view.

Nicole has a visual identity that moves comfortably between relaxed, expressive and high-fashion imagery. Her photographs offer contrast: casual denim, natural light and understated styling on one side; dramatic beauty, strong styling and monochrome editorial on the other.

What connects those different looks is the person in the frame. Her individual style, tattoos and expressive features give photographers and creative teams a distinctive starting point.

Nicole's portfolio is an exploration of visual character, personal style and the different directions a single face can take.

Her visual language.

Individuality is not something to style out.

  • Expressive — distinctive features and a strong visual presence.
  • Editorial — suited to considered styling, creative direction and image-led concepts.
  • Unconventional — tattoos, personal style and an appearance that does not disappear into the background.
  • Versatile in presentation — from relaxed, natural imagery to more dramatic and glamorous styling.
